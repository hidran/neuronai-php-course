# Cedar FAQ

Q: Can I change my delivery address?
A: Yes, until the parcel leaves the warehouse. After that, please contact the carrier directly.

Q: Do you ship outside the country?
A: Not yet. We only deliver to addresses inside the country.

Q: How do I use a gift card?
A: Enter the code at checkout. Any remaining balance stays on the card for later orders.

Q: Can I cancel an order?
A: Yes, within one hour of payment. After that, the order is already being packed.
