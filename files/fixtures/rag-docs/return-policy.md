# Cedar Support: returning a damaged item

Customers may return a damaged item within 17 days of delivery. A photo showing the damage is required with the return request. Contact Cedar Support before sending the damaged item back.

## Refunds

A refund is issued to the original payment method after the warehouse confirms the damage. Refunds usually appear within five business days, depending on the card issuer.

## Exchanges

If the same item is still in stock, we can ship a replacement instead of issuing a refund. The replacement follows the same 17-day window and the same photo requirement.

## What is not covered

Normal wear, accidental damage after delivery, and items bought from a third-party seller are outside this policy. This document describes damaged item returns only.

## Response times

Cedar Support answers a damage claim within two business days. Keep the original packaging until the claim is closed, because the carrier may ask for a photo of the box as well as the item.

## Shipping the item back

Cedar Support sends a prepaid label for damaged returns. Drop the parcel at any partner point and keep the receipt until the refund is confirmed. If the label does not arrive within one business day, contact support again with the claim number.
