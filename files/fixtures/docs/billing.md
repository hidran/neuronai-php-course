# Tidepool billing guide

This guide explains invoices, plans and payment methods.

## Invoices

Customers find every invoice under Account, then Billing,
then Invoices. Each invoice can be downloaded as a PDF for
twelve months after the payment.

## Plans and payments

Tidepool has three plans: Starter, Team and Business. The
Starter plan covers one workspace, the Team plan covers up
to ten workspaces, and the Business plan has no workspace
limit. Every plan includes the same sync features.

Plans are billed monthly or yearly. A yearly plan is billed
once, on the day the subscription starts, and it costs the
same as ten monthly payments. Switching from monthly to
yearly takes effect on the next billing date.

Tidepool accepts credit cards and bank transfers. A bank
transfer must arrive within ten days of the invoice date,
otherwise the workspace becomes read only until the payment
is received. Card payments are charged on the billing date.
